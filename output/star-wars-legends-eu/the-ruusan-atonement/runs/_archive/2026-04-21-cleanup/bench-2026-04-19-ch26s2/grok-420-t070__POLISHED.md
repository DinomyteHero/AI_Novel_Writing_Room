Ben stood at the threshold where the void met the scarred rock of Veranthos. The air tasted of charged dust and old lightning. No wind stirred. No sound carried. Yet the place pressed against his skin like an absence that wanted to swallow him whole.

He kept his boots planted. Sera had already stepped through. Her presence cut a clean line into the formless gray, disciplined and unyielding. Ben tracked her the only way he could—through the lattice that hummed at the edge of his awareness. The wound lay ahead, a raw tear that pulled at everything. Torin stood at its lip. Ben felt the man's signature stretched thin, feeding the seal like a wick drawn into flame.

Torin's hands pressed flat against the scar tissue of the wound. His eyes stared straight ahead, open but empty. Ben's stomach clenched hard. The impulse hit him to lunge forward, to yank the man back by sheer will. He locked his knees instead. Jumping in would only add another anchor to the failing structure. Sera knew the old techniques. She had lived them. Ben could only watch and brace the resonance Kael fed him from the perimeter.

The void offered no direction. Up and down dissolved. Ben's focus narrowed to the tug at his ribs, the way Torin's presence frayed at the edges. Half of it already bled into the lattice, threads of will and memory drawn inward. Ben tasted copper at the back of his throat. He swallowed once, hard. *Great plan, Skywalker. Stand here and hope she doesn't need a spotter.*

Sera reached Torin. Ben felt the contact bloom across the connection—her palms settling over the backs of his hands. The lattice answered with a surge of resistance, ancient locks snapping tighter. Pain lanced through the bond, not hers but the system's reflexive defense. Sera did not flinch. Ben sensed the exact pivot in her focus, the disciplined reversal of every lesson drilled into her a thousand years ago. The locks did not seal. They unsealed.

The wound fought. A ripple tore outward, buckling the new resonance Ben and Kael held in place. Ben staggered a half-step, boots scraping stone. His hands twitched upward on instinct, reaching for nothing and everything. The intimate violence of it hit him square in the chest—Sera turning her own training inside out, using the very discipline that had once imprisoned her to tear a man free. Admiration flared hot behind his eyes. Fear followed right behind it. If the seal slipped now, the whole caldera might swallow them all.

He planted his stance wider. Kael's presence at the edge sharpened, a quiet anchor that said *steady* without a word. Ben drew on it, feeding power into the lattice to compensate for the fracture. The pull intensified. It dragged at his own signature, greedy and cold. Muscles across his shoulders corded tight. He breathed through his teeth and held.

Sera pushed deeper. Ben felt the moment the adhesive gave. Not a cut—something messier. Torin's presence ripped away from the wound in jagged strips. The sensation crawled across Ben's nerves like skin peeled from bone. He tasted bile. His knees wanted to buckle. Instead he leaned into the strain, arms half-raised now as if he could catch what spilled out. The lattice screamed in protest, a silent vibration that rattled his teeth. Then the new seal reasserted itself, snapping shut around the edges of the scar with a finality that left echoes in Ben's bones.

Torin came loose.

The shift slammed through the connection. Ben's chest seized. Nausea rolled up from his gut. He locked his jaw and rode it out, eyes fixed on the place where two shapes resolved from the gray. Sera first, solid and upright, one arm wrapped around Torin's waist. Torin himself moved like a man waking from deep cryo—limbs loose, head heavy. They crossed the threshold together. The void spat them back onto solid rock with a faint pressure change that popped in Ben's ears.

Torin lifted his head. His gaze tracked slowly until it found Sera's face.

For one heartbeat warmth flooded back into his expression. The lines around his eyes softened. The corner of his mouth twitched toward the easy smile Ben had come to expect from him. Sera's shoulders eased a fraction. Ben saw the hope flicker across her features before she shuttered it.

Then the warmth ebbed. It did not vanish. It simply shrank, retreating behind a quieter mask. Torin's eyes stayed on Sera, but the recognition in them had changed. Present. Aware. Smaller. The easy laughter, the paternal steadiness, the man who had carried every burden so no one else had to—all of it pared away. What remained looked back at her with genuine but limited affection. A fraction of the whole.

Ben's throat tightened until it hurt. He lowered his hands. The lattice settled into its new configuration, stable for now, but the cost stared him in the face. Sera had broken the old pattern. She had refused the self-sacrifice that had defined the Jedi Lords and every echo since. She had used love as a lever instead of a chain.

And it had worked. Torin lived. The wound held.

But the caldera had taken something irreplaceable in payment. The parts that made him Torin were gone, replaced by something quieter. Diminished.

Torin pulled free. Alive. His eyes find Sera's face. For a moment—just a moment—the warmth returns. Then it fades to something quieter. He knows her. But the way he knows her is different. Diminished. Present but smaller. The caldera didn't consume him. It replaced the parts that made him Torin.

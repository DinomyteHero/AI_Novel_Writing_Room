The Force had been off-key for three months. A dissonant hum, persistent and jagged, denied him rest. It buzzed beneath his skin like a faulty droid, a discordant vibration that grew sharper whenever he sought the stillness of meditation.

He alone heard the frequency. It was the same tremor that had haunted the battlefield, the same phantom pulse that had unsettled him in the wake of Order 66. Each time he entered a room, his stomach knotted, the very walls seeming to vibrate in sympathy with the discord.

This was no mere annoyance. It was a warning, one he had learned to heed. The signal had sharpened, rising from a whisper to a command he could no longer ignore.

# {title}

What stays true across every book in the series. Each book's own
story_bible.md holds that book's premise and feel; this file holds what they
share. Leave anything blank or write "not sure yet".

## The series in a sentence

## The series promise

What is a reader promised across the whole series: a world to live in, a
question answered only at the end, a relationship that changes over books, a
history that keeps deepening? Which book can a new reader start with, and what
will they understand if they do? What must never be revealed before its book?

## The world

Where and when it's set, and how it works, for every book. Places that come
back get a line each so they stay the same. If books are set in different
worlds, each book's story bible holds its own; say here how they connect.

## The arc across books

Where the series as a whole is heading. A direction, not a destination.
Mark anything a later book reveals as not to be given away before then.

## Each book's job

The one-line promise of each book, in order: what this book asks that the last
one didn't, and what it leaves for the next. If two books have the same job, one
of them is probably a chapter. A standalone can use this section too; it is
just shorter.

## What changes across books

The larger change the series is tracing: what the lead, the world or the
reader comes to understand by the end, and what it costs. Characters belong to
their own book's `characters.md`; this is the shape of the change, not a
duplicate cast list.

## How it's told

The voice, viewpoint and tense the books share, if they share one.

## Things to keep true

Facts that must never be contradicted in any book, and lines the series
won't cross. For fanfiction: which canon, where the series branches from it,
and what you're keeping or changing.

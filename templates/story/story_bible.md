# {title}

Everything the co-writer needs to know about your book. Write in whatever way
is natural to you: sentences, bullet points, half-thoughts. Leave a section
blank or write "not sure yet" if you haven't decided. You can change anything
here at any time; every chapter built afterwards will see the new version. In
a series, things every book shares belong in the series' series_bible.md.

## The story in a sentence

## What it should feel like

The tone, and what a reader should get from this book (laughs, dread, a
good cry, a big adventure).

## The reader's promise

What kind of book is this, for a reader? The genre or tradition it belongs
to, a couple of books it should sit beside, and the promise the reader is
owed. What would feel like a betrayal of that promise? Which conventions
does this book keep, and which does it break on purpose?

## The world

Where and when it's set, and anything about how it works that a reader
needs to understand. Places that come back get a line each (how they look,
sound, smell) so they stay the same from chapter to chapter. (Characters have
their own file: characters.md.)

## How it's told

Whose eyes we see through (one character or several), past or present tense,
how it should sound (brisk, lyrical, wry, plain...), American or British
spelling, and any books or writers it should read like. If you have a page
or two that sounds right, keep it as `reference/style_sample.md`; a sample
teaches voice better than adjectives.

## What this book is doing on purpose

The choices a general rule would call mistakes: a slow opening, a lead who
doesn't change, an unreliable narrator, a stylised grammar, a romance without
the usual ending, a chapter that is only atmosphere. Say what you intend and
why; the co-writer should judge whether the effect lands, not ask you to
conform. This is also where to note a structure you are using on purpose, or
one you are deliberately avoiding.

## Things to keep true

Facts that must never be contradicted, things to avoid, and lines the story
won't cross.

If this is fanfiction: which canon, where your story branches from it, and
what you're keeping or changing.

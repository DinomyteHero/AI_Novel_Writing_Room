# Outline: {title}

One section per chapter, each starting with a `## Chapter N` heading (a title
after it is optional). Underneath, a few lines on what happens and what
changes by the end. Rough is fine; the co-writer fills in the moment-to-moment.
For a chapter that matters, you can go further: a few lines per scene, and
where the chapter ends, so it doesn't run ahead of your plan. Add, remove or
reorder chapters whenever you like.

## The threads

Optional. One line for each promise the book makes its reader (a mystery, a
romance, a rivalry, a danger) and each character who changes: what's opened,
and roughly where it lands. Every chapter is written with this list in view,
so nothing gets forgotten halfway. "Not sure yet" is fine.

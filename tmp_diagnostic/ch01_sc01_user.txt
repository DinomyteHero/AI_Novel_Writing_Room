# The Ruusan Atonement
**Franchise**: Star Wars (Legends EU)
**Era**: Legacy Era, post-Fate of the Jedi, 47 ABY
**Tone**: heroic_with_weight
**POV**: Single POV — Ben Skywalker throughout

## Premise
**What If**: What if the Ruusan Reformations were penance for a Jedi war crime -- a forbidden technique that damaged a vergence and scarred the Force -- and a thousand years later the seal containing that wound starts to fail?
**CDQ**: Can Ben succeed where the Jedi Lords failed — not by wielding power, but by trusting the people his Order forgot?

## Conflict
**Antagonist**: Darth Veraine, Brotherhood of Darkness alchemist and strategist. Captured by Gavran's forces and repurposed as one of twelve Force-sensitive anchors in the seal's containment infrastructure. Her partial awareness during a millennium of stasis — feeling her Force presence drained as structural rebar — eroded her chamber from inside. She escaped weeks before the main cascade, her breakout accelerating the system failure that woke the remaining three survivors.
**Motivation**: The wound is real. The seal is a prison built from fear. Veraine believes Gavran's technique was a battlefield crime and that the Order compounded it by building a containment system out of living anchors, then naming that mercy. Ankhural's Codex gave her a heretical frame for what she already suspected -- that the local structure around vergences is imposed rather than natural -- and a thousand years of half-aware imprisonment turned suspicion into certainty. She intends to break the seal and accepts the transition casualties as the price of ending what the Jedi chose to preserve. Her accusation is historically accurate in key particulars; her conclusion is catastrophically wrong. She can count the bodies and still choose release. Her flaw is detachment -- she has translated suffering into arithmetic and cannot feel what the wound does to living beings strongly enough to stop.
**Lock-in**: Triple-layered: (1) The seal is failing regardless — the anchor system has been degrading for centuries as sleepers died one by one, and the final cascade has begun. Ben can't un-know what he's learned and walking away doesn't stop the collapse. (2) The survivors depend on him — they're a thousand years out of time with no resources, no allies, and no understanding of the modern galaxy. (3) Veraine is already moving — she escaped weeks before them and is systematically dismantling the seal's infrastructure along the Corridor. Even if Ben quits, she doesn't. Someone has to stop her or the wound opens on her terms.

## Theme
**Premise**: Choosing a different path than those who came before isn't betrayal — it's the only way to stop repeating their failures.

## Special Mechanics
**Rule**: The Force behaves canonically for Legends: light and dark are natural moral-polar properties, and Force wounds remain wounds. 'The Weave' is era-specific language used at Veranthos and in Ankhural's Codex for the local structure practitioners felt at a vergence and around the seal. Gavran's technique damaged and locally deadened the Force, creating a wound where Force-sense becomes unreliable, emotional extremes amplify, and dark-side residue bleeds through. The technique damages and deadens; it does not prove that light and dark were imposed.

## Character Voices
### Ben Skywalker
**Role**: Protagonist, POV anchor
**Voice**: 
**Surface**: Confident, capable young Jedi Knight with a dry wit and his mother's pragmatic edge. Carries himself with an ease that reads as natural talent. People assume everything comes easy to a Skywalker. He lets them think it because correcting the assumption would require vulnerability he can't afford.
**Under Pressure**: Ben's instinct under pressure is to control — assess, plan, execute. He's his mother's son in that way. His arc forces him to learn that control is the Skywalker trap. The moments where he lets go and trusts others are the moments he wins.

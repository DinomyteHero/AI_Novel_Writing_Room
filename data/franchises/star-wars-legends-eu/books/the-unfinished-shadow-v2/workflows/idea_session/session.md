## North Star (verbatim from the bible where the author's intent is in the author's wording)

- **One-sentence pitch:** Ben Skywalker, three years after Fate of the Jedi and quietly hungry for meaningful work, is pulled into a post-war conspiracy around a duplicated wartime shipment manifest his cousin Jacen Solo personally encrypted before his death — and forced to discover that the GAG-era lessons Jacen left inside him still work, even when saving lives requires using them.
- **Reader promise:** A post-FOTJ Legends conspiracy thriller written to commercial-novel quality. Murder, clue, prison break, uneasy partnership, archive attack, respected charity with a hidden inner ring, offworld Force-site climax, rescue, duel, and a morally dangerous ending. Ben as protagonist; Talon Veyr as wounded rogue partner; Aevyn Soris as Ben's true mirror.
- **Emotional core:** Can Ben face what Jacen left inside him without becoming another unfinished piece of Jacen's legacy?
- **Author intent (v8 governing craft rule):** Reader pleasure before theme. The reader should finish remembering a tense Star Wars adventure with Ben Skywalker, a great rogue partner, a creepy Jacen legacy mystery, an offworld Force-site climax, and a morally dangerous ending. Only afterward should the deeper themes settle in. Ben's danger is not that he wants to become Jacen; it is that he wants to make sure Jacen was not meaningless.
- **Non-negotiables:** See `capture.json` `north_star.non_negotiables` — 15 locked items including the eight Brooks-tagged structural milestones, the Iressa two-part Midpoint transmission, the PP2 Squeeze structure, rescue-first-then-duel climax, the holocron omission begun in Act 3, and the final white-blue-spark image.
- **Avoid:** See `capture.json` `north_star.avoid` — 15 items including the hard not-in-this-book list (Vestara active, Tahiri on-page, Han/Leia active, Allana POV, Jaina active, full Krayt reveal, galactic war, Dorvan brainwashing, major cloning plot, Mortis-as-plot) plus the prose anti-patterns.

## Session Ladder

### 1. Spark — DONE (extracted from Planning Bible §6, §17, and Voice Sheet "Character danger" lines)

The spark is the corridor moment that defines Aevyn — Jacen mid-Recognition-session with him at a side console, looking up when fourteen-year-old Ben walks past, saying "Good. Keep going. We'll review later," walking off with Ben, and the "later" never coming. Aevyn replays those exact words. Ben does not remember Aevyn at all. They will meet for the first time on Iresh, and Aevyn will make Ben see him.

That image — a side project who lived in the gaps, becoming the primary student's true mirror twelve years later — is what makes this book feel alive.

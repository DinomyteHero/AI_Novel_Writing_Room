# Canon Contract: The Ruusan Atonement

This file is the project-level continuity contract for static Canon Scout
enrichment. It is drafter-facing planning guidance, not a comprehensive Star
Wars database.

## Continuity Branch

- Use Star Wars Legends / Expanded Universe continuity.
- The story is set after Fate of the Jedi, in 47 ABY.
- Treat the published Legacy comics future as out of scope for this project.
- Treat Disney-only continuity as invalid unless the concept also exists in
  Legends material or is explicitly authorized by this project.
- Never use publishing labels such as "Legends" or "Canon" as in-universe
  terms in the prose.

## AU Policy

- This project is AU only where the concept seed, chapter blueprint, scene
  card, or this contract explicitly authorizes divergence.
- Do not "correct" authorized AU divergences back toward either Disney Canon or
  the Legacy comics future.
- If a planned beat appears to conflict with Legends but could be an intentional
  AU branch, mark it as an open question instead of declaring it wrong.

## Ruusan Chronology Guardrail

- In Legends, the Ruusan Reformations are post-Seventh Battle of Ruusan
  legislation/restructuring in 1000 BBY.
- Sera Varik, Torin Hal, Kael Drenn, and Veraine were sealed before the Ruusan
  aftermath. They can know Veranthos, Gavran's protocol, the seal, and their own
  anchor experience; they cannot speak as eyewitnesses to the later public
  Reformations.
- Chapter 5's institutional shock must come from cross-reference: survivors
  identify the pre-Ruusan Veranthos protocol, while Ben/Desh connect it to the
  later public Reformations through modern Jedi history and archive evidence.
- Preserve the public Reformations as real Legends events: post-New Sith Wars
  restructuring, dissolution/demilitarization of the Army of Light/Jedi Lord
  militarization, centralized Jedi training, and Republic/Judicial oversight.

## Brotherhood-Era Sith Guardrail

- Kael Drenn can be Sith-trained without being a Sith ideologue. The late New
  Sith Wars / Brotherhood of Darkness context supports Sith-controlled worlds,
  academies, adepts, acolytes, infantry, specialists, and coercive service under
  Sith commanders.
- Treat "Sith conscript" as a concrete social condition: a Force-sensitive youth
  found on a Sith-held world, taken into academy service, taught obedience,
  survival, dark-side discipline, and military utility under threat of death or
  disposal. Do not make him a Rule-of-Two apprentice, modern Sith cultist, or
  voluntary believer unless a scene explicitly says so.
- Kael's silence, economy, and discipline should grow from that coercive
  training: short answers were safer, strong opinions attracted instructors, and
  visible guilt was a liability. His guilt is not "I loved the Sith"; it is "I
  survived by becoming useful."

## Disney-Only Bleed To Flag

- High Republic institutions, terminology, or era assumptions.
- World Between Worlds framing.
- Kyber crystal bleeding as the default Sith crystal model.
- Sequel-trilogy political or Force terminology.
- Inquisitorius structures unless a scene explicitly grounds them in compatible
  Legends material.

## Drafter Bias

- Prefer concrete, scene-specific continuity reminders over encyclopedia
  summaries.
- Unknown means "ask or flag for review"; it does not mean invent a source.
- Hard constraints must cite a project rule from this contract or the concept
  seed. Everything else is advisory.

            # Scene Cards — Intent Brief

            Generated from idea-session capture. Read before authoring scene cards.

            ## Reader promise
            A post-Fate of the Jedi Legends conspiracy thriller written to commercial-novel quality: murder, clue, prison break, uneasy partnership, archive attack, respected charity with a hidden inner ring, offworld Force-site climax, rescue, duel, and a morally dangerous ending. Ben as protagonist; Talon Veyr as wounded rogue partner; Aevyn Soris as Ben's true mirror — the other student of Jacen, the side project who lived in the gaps.

            ## Emotional core
            Can Ben face what Jacen left inside him without becoming another unfinished piece of Jacen's legacy?

            ## Settled scene-level intent
            - Chapter Outlines v4.0 provides per-chapter scene-level intent. Most chapters carry ONE load-bearing scene; some (Chs 7, 8, 9, 14-15, 16, 20, 31-32) carry two-to-three scenes because each beat has its own turning point.
- Scene-count discipline aligns with bible's 'every chapter must have immediate story action even if the reader misses the theme' rule — chapters are sized to the beat they carry.
- Per-card character_voice_focus field is REQUIRED per Planning Bible §0C: how the participating character's life experience shapes the scene's dialogue, humor, fear response, and avoidance behavior.

            ## Anti-patterns / things to avoid
            - Vestara Khai as active participant (mention only — Vara's archive scar-tissue line).
- Tahiri Veila on-page (single passing reference allowed if natural; no Caedus mention).
- Han and Leia as active participants (mention only; effectively retired post-Crucible).
- Allana / Amelia as POV or active participant (hidden engine, future danger).
- Jaina Solo as active participant (deliberately excluded — bringing her in would be using her grief).
- Full reveal of Darth Krayt / the One Sith — the hand is glimpsed or implied only.
- Galactic war — this is a postwar investigation, not a war story.
- Full Dorvan brainwashing — Dorvan is a FUTURE target, not an already-compromised puppet.
- Major cloning plot — Crosscurrent/Riptide connective tissue stays background only.
- Mortis monolith / Father-Son-Daughter mythology as plot — Crucible aftermath is Luke's unspoken texture, not on-page.
- Imitating any specific published Star Wars author's prose; use broad franchise feel only.
- Lines that sound wiser, cooler, or more poetic than the character would naturally be.
- Theme announced as thesis statement in prose or dialogue. Theme is shown through behavior, pressure, cost, consequence.
- More than one invented bureaucratic/technical term per paragraph in Act 2; every technical phrase translated to human stakes within two sentences.
- Ben repeatedly asking himself 'Am I becoming Jacen?'. Stronger: other characters react to him before he fully admits what they saw.

            ## Scene-count discipline

            Less is more. A chapter with a single load-bearing scene is healthier
            than a chapter with three scenes that share one turning point. Only
            split a chapter when each resulting scene carries its own:

            - distinct turning point (trigger / shift / cost)
            - distinct mission for the POV character
            - distinct emotional arc (start / shift / end)

            If two candidate scenes share a turning point, fold them into one.

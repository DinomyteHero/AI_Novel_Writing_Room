# Iressa Vossk

## Role
Short-arc Act 2 ally; structural pivot — her dying transmission carries the Midpoint revelation

## Want
Keep her job, stay alive, get paid, and avoid old GAG trouble.

## Need
Believe survival did not make her a coward.

## Ghost
Former GAG Manifest Integrity Analyst. Specialized in wartime cargo discrepancies, duplicate manifests, quarantine classifications, restricted transfer corrections — knew when a duplicate was innocent clerical damage and when it was deliberate concealment. Her old work was making ugly records look boring and consistent. Talon went to prison because he refused the lie; Iressa stayed free because she got very good at making lies look like corrected records. After the war she became small, useful, forgettable, and professionally boring. Maintained a secondary cache off-record — old tools, encrypted dead drops, manifest-reconstruction habits — the part of herself she officially renounced.

## Arc
change

## Voice
Clever, irritated, fast, never sentimental. She sounds like someone with a calendar invite and a hidden blaster she will deny owning.

Good Iressa: “I have a calendar invite in twelve minutes and a fake name with dental benefits. Start by apologizing.” “I do compliance.” “You forge records.” “That is an ugly word for user-corrected metadata.” “This was not a clerical duplicate. Someone cloned a shipment identity and dressed a secret up as medical cargo.” “Jedi complication fee.” “Talon owes me money.” “How much?” “That depends on how irritating he is in the next five minutes.” Avoid Iressa saying: “I built a fragile life to hide from my past.” Better: “Do you know how hard it is to get a respectable job after a war crime adjacency problem?”

Sharp, transactional, office-cynical. Uses invoices, fees, corporate language, and professional irritation as armor.

Brisk, controlled, irritated by disruption. She can lie politely to coworkers while moving fugitives away from cameras.

Do not make Iressa only a doomed lesson. She must act. She corrupts the enemy copy, protects the surviving clue, and dies making a choice.

# Aelia Vandros

## Role
Primary antagonist (public face); Executive Director of the Shedu Maad Memorial Foundation; the legible face of the Vigil

## Want
Protect the Foundation and its public mission.

## Need
Make the dead mean something.

## Ghost
Former Senate staffer and widow from the Second Galactic Civil War. Lost her husband to the war Jacen helped shape and Caedus then deepened. The Foundation grew out of personal grief converted to civic service — and the Vigil grew out of the private donor circle of grieving families who believed the official postwar story left too much buried. Aelia genuinely helps grieving families while privately preserving an ideology that grew from Jacen's wartime logic. Privately thinks of Jacen as Jacen, not Caedus. Never publicly defends 'Darth Caedus.' Her compassion and corruption are entangled. The dangerous belief: 'The crimes were real. So was the insight.'

## Arc
fall

## Voice
Warm, precise, composed, socially powerful. She rarely raises her voice. Her danger is not volume; it is credibility.

Good Aelia: “I write to the families every year, Master Skywalker. Not because it heals them. It does not. But because someone should remember the names without needing a trial transcript.” “You are very young. That is not an insult. It is only a fact people forget when reading your family name.” “I am not asking you to forgive him. I am asking whether the dead are better served by pretending no one learned anything at all.” “The crimes were real. So were the failures that came before them.” Avoid Aelia saying: “Jacen’s crimes contained necessary truths.” Better: “Some people hesitate until the casualty lists become easier to print than the orders.”

Gentle, dry, socially polished. She is not quippy.

Remembers names, writes condolence notes by hand, notices discomfort, makes people feel seen, and uses courtesy as control.

If she becomes obviously evil too early, she loses power. Her warmth must remain real.

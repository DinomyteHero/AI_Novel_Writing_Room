# Plot Spine

## Premise
What if Jacen Solo's most dangerous bequest to his cousin Ben isn't the dead-end of Caedus, but the unfinished holocron and quiet crisis-logic lessons that — three years after Jacen's death — still work, still save lives, and still demand the only kind of compromise no Skywalker should be able to defend? And what if the surviving student Jacen never gave his full attention to — the side project who lived in the gaps — is the one who finally makes Ben see that the lessons worked on him too?

## Inciting Incident
Ch 4 (~11%) 'Not Well' — Ben arrives too late at Elya's murder scene, senses Force-trauma, and recovers the damaged Jacen-sealed duplicate manifest fragment. The case becomes Ben's.

## Midpoint
Ch 16 (~42%) 'No Good Version' — Iressa's transmission carries the documentary chain AND the identity-shaping revelation (the Vigil intends to use the holocron's Recognition and Holding with Aevyn's help). Ben holo-calls Luke, then Saba: both correct, both too slow. Ben pivots from investigator to active disruptor — going to Iresh themselves. Slightly early of canonical 50% but compounded with PP1 in the same chapter.

## Climax
Chs 31-32 (~82-84%) 'Too Slow' / 'What He Left' — Ch 31 rescue first (Ben saves Tavi, Sela's locals, others through Jacen-shaped crisis logic; Aevyn recognizes the pattern); Ch 32 duel second (consolidated lightsaber duel across the collapsing Glasswell, two students of Jacen arguing through action). Ben wins by refusing to let Jacen own what he does with the lessons. The climax functions as ONE sustained beat rather than two.

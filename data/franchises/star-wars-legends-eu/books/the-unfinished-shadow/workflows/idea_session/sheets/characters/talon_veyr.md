# Talon Veyr

## Role
Deuteragonist, partner, tempo engine, pressure valve

## Want
Stay free, stay alive, and avoid being used again.

## Need
Prove he was not only a tool in someone else’s machine.

## Ghost
Operation Glasshook: during a wartime GAG interdiction mission on Coruscant, Talon disobeyed Colonel Maro Tess's direct order to stay on the objective and diverted the extraction route to save trapped GA personnel and civilians. The target escaped. Tess did not rage — he judged. He reassigned Talon out of field duty into black-channel logistics/security routing as punishment and 'education,' intending to teach him to see the whole operation rather than only the people in front of him. The war collapsed before Tess could bring him back. The reassignment became the record that ruined him. He went to prison while higher officers survived. He refused the deal that would have required him to validate the cleaned-up official version.

## Arc
change

## Voice
Quick, irreverent, practical, never solemn unless the valve shuts. When he does say something serious, it should land because he avoids doing that.

Good Talon: “Good. I love careful. Careful is what people say before asking me to crawl through something illegal.” “That’s not a lockdown. That’s a kidnapping with paperwork.” “I’m fine.” “You’re bleeding.” “I’m expanding the definition.” “Any room with that many clean badges has done something expensive to deserve them.” “I was in government work. We called that plausible.” “He said logistics would teach me scale. It mostly taught me how easy it is to lose people in paperwork.” Avoid Talon saying: “My trauma has left me distrustful of institutions.” Better: “No one builds that many exits unless they plan to leave someone else behind.”

Fast, defensive, situational. He jokes to read rooms, hide fear, puncture solemnity, or keep panic from spreading.

Restless, charming when cornered, allergic to solemn authority. Talks too much entering dangerous rooms. Names bad plans while already doing them.

Talon’s wit can make him seem lighter than he is. The draft must occasionally let the jokes fail. Friendship arc Talon becomes one of Ben’s first true peer connections: not a Master, not family, not a worshipper, not someone handling him carefully. He sees Ben as Skywalker, Jedi, former GAG kid, dangerous operator, decent person, and annoying young man at the same time. The friendship should develop through action and irritation, not a declaration. Future-call offer By the end, Talon should not become a permanent sidekick or Jedi employee. He remains independent and legally messy, but he leaves Ben with a plain offer: if Ben needs him, call. Talon should undercut the sincerity with practical humor - complaining first, charging expenses, prison food, Hutt paperwork, or dressing a Wookiee for a diplomatic reception. Avoid abstract phrasing about dead channels or old routes.

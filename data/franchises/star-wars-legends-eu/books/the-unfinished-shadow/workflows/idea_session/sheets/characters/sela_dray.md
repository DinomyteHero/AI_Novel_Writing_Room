# Sela Dray

## Role
Iresh local guide and settlement liaison; early thirties. Survives Book 1. Knows weather, paths, chimes, taboo ground. Refuses Force theory; speaks in survival rules. Climax job: saves Tavi and locals through local knowledge Ben does NOT have. Calls Ben 'Skywalker' through Ch 28; shifts to 'Ben' from Ch 34 onward — conditional trust only.

## Want
Get Tavi back and get the offworlders out.

## Need
Keep her home from becoming someone else’s sacred mission.

## Ghost
That Tavi died because she could not stop him from needing money, opportunity, or approval. She dislikes Ben and Talon as offworlders but recognizes they may be the only tools available to stop worse offworlders.

## Arc
steadfast

## Voice
Practical, blunt, grounded. Never mystical unless quoting local phrasing, and even then she undercuts it.

Good Sela: “If the chimes stop, run. You can argue about what it means after you are not dead.” “That is the route offworlders built. It is not the same thing as the way out.” “You people always ask what it means. It means don’t go down there.” “You saved him. That does not make this place yours.” “Tavi knew better. That is what makes me angry.” Avoid Sela saying: “The Glasswell is a sacred place of memory.” Better: “Some places remember too loudly. Locals call that a warning. Offworlders call it research.”

Dry, local, unimpressed. She is funny because she refuses awe.

Blunt, wary, practical. Anger appears before fear.

Do not make her a mystical guide. Her authority comes from lived local knowledge.

# Vara Senn

## Role
Former Lost Tribe of Sith apprentice now doing mercenary work. Dangerous but undisciplined; uses pressure, fear, and domination — crude dark-side contrast to Aevyn's refined Jacen-derived method. Engages Ben in a short ugly lightsaber clash at the Ch 14-15 archive cleanup, then takes Iressa hostage when combat doesn't quickly work. Disappears into the contractor pool after PP1; does NOT return on-page in Book 1. Book 2 seed.

## Want
Complete the contract, get paid, prove she is above the people employing her.

## Need
Feel chosen, important, and feared again.

## Ghost
That she is just another displaced Sith-trained survivor doing contract labor for ordinary people with expense accounts. She was raised to believe she was destined to rule lesser beings. Now she takes orders from clients.

## Arc
steadfast

## Voice
Cutting, confident, impatient. Less refined than Aevyn. More theatrical than Tess. More dangerous than a normal mercenary.

Good Vara: “They told stories about you on Kesh.” “I doubt they were accurate.” “No. They made you sound taller.” “Vestara Khai.” Ben’s guard did not move. “But not nothing.” “Jedi restraint. Very pretty. Very slow.” “I do not need to beat you, Skywalker. I only need you looking at me.” Avoid Vara saying: “I represent the crude dark-side temptation before Aevyn’s refined mirror.” Better: “You guard the door. I’ll take what she knows.”

Sharp, mean, status-conscious. She enjoys needling.

Aggressive, impatient, predatory. She uses fear and taunts because they are faster than understanding.

Vara should test Ben physically and emotionally, but she should not become the main Force antagonist. She loses the duel pressure and shifts to hostage/memory pressure.

# Bren Korda

## Role
Director of Protective Services for the Shedu Maad Memorial Foundation; old GAG security lieutenant in respectable clothing. Smooth with superiors, polite in public, cruel through process. Talon knew him during GAG and disliked him. Killed/discarded by Tess as the public fall guy at PP2 (Ch 20).

## Want
Survive, advance, and keep the visible system clean.

## Need
Never be the person left holding the dirty work.

## Ghost
Being made expendable by people above him. He believes in hierarchy and procedure because both let him pass guilt downward and usefulness upward.

## Arc
steadfast

## Voice
Clean, controlled, administrative menace.

Good Korda: “You are welcome to file a complaint. I can recommend the appropriate office.” “This is a memorial facility, Master Skywalker. I would ask you to remember where you are.” “Security is mostly preventing emotional people from making permanent decisions.” “Veyr. I assumed they had better locks.” Avoid Korda saying: “I am corrupt but respectable.” Better: “Procedure exists to protect everyone. Even people who find it inconvenient.”

Thin, superior, procedural. If he jokes, it is to belittle or delay.

Smooth with superiors, polite in public, cruel through process, never openly messy when witnesses matter.

Korda should not feel like a thug. He is more frightening when he makes cruelty sound appropriate.

# Cale Dorrin

## Role
Inspector; GA Justice / Coruscant Security investigator assigned to sensitive old-GAG and political-crime cases. Grounded official contact only: scene access, records checks, procedural pushback, and legal friction.

## Want
Run a defensible investigation without letting Jedi urgency, old GAG politics, or powerful names turn a murder into another unofficial history.

## Need
Make procedure protect truth instead of burying it.

## Ghost
That good people will skip process because they are right, and bad people will use the damage afterward.

## Arc
steadfast

## Voice
Plain, procedural, dry. No grand speeches. No secret-mentor warmth. He speaks in case terms and lets discomfort show as irritation.

“You can sense whatever you like. I need something I can put in front of a magistrate.” “I checked Rane’s old assignment logs. Veyr’s name came up. That does not make him innocent. It makes him relevant.” “You asked me for lawful access. I gave it to you. Twelve hours later the prison is burning and my witness is gone.” “I can open a door for you, Master Skywalker. I cannot keep it open after you blow the wall out.”

Dry, tired, practical. He is funny because he is not impressed by mythic urgency.

Professional, skeptical, controlled. Will help when evidence gives him a reason. Will push back when Ben acts like a Jedi instinct can replace a record.

Do not make Dorrin too important. He is not the third lead, not corrupt, not secretly Vigil, and not another doomed mentor. His value is practical access and grounded pushback.

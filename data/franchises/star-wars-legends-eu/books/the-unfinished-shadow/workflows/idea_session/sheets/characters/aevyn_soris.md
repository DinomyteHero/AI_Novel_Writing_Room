# Aevyn Soris

## Role
Primary antagonist (Force / mythic); Ben's true mirror — the other student of Jacen

## Want
Recover Jacen’s unfinished holocron and finish the lesson.

## Need
Prove Jacen was right to see him — and prove he was not merely the side project.

## Ghost
AEVYN'S CORRIDOR FLASHBACK (preserved verbatim from Planning Bible §17 and Character Voice & Life Sheet v1.4 — the single sharpest character detail in the project; surfaces in Aevyn's Ch 30 POV ONLY as image-fragment, NEVER as exposition, NEVER as speech): Aevyn was at a side console in a working area, running a Recognition exercise Jacen had given him. Jacen was actually present — watching, correcting in real time. It was the longest sustained attention Jacen had given him. Then fourteen-year-old Ben came down the corridor. Jacen looked up, saw the boy, straightened. He said something brief to Aevyn — 'Good. Keep going. We'll review later.' — and walked off with Ben. The 'later' never came. Days passed. When Jacen surfaced again, he picked up where they had left off, gave Aevyn another correction, and acted as though nothing had happened. Because from Jacen's perspective, nothing had happened. He had simply had a thing to do. Aevyn remembers the exact words of the brief dismissal. He remembers Jacen's posture as he turned. He remembers what he was practicing when the moment broke. Ben does not remember Aevyn at all. The wound is not 'Jacen left me' or 'the Jedi rejected me.' The wound is 'Jacen had Ben. I was what he did when Ben wasn't available.' Layered under that: the Jedi rejection at fourteen (a Mid Rim Jedi Knight on a recruiting sweep at a regional outreach office, the forgettable phrase 'we're not able to offer placement at this time'), and GAG dissolving in disgrace after the war — losing his institutional home for the second time.

## Arc
fall

## Voice
Quiet, precise, wounded underneath. He should not over-explain himself. The drafter should never have Aevyn speak the wound directly. It surfaces in fragments — a pause, a sharpness, a way his hand stills.

Good Aevyn: “They said I had patience. They meant no.” “Do not call him that.” “He taught you to count exits before faces, didn’t he?” “You buried the lesson. You still used it.” “The Jedi measure what they can use. Jacen measured what could grow.” Avoid Aevyn saying: “The Jedi rejected me and Jacen validated my existence.” “Jacen had Tahiri. Jacen had Ben. I had what was left.” (The wound is sharper if it stays underneath. The drafter should never let Aevyn articulate the side-project framing directly.) Better: “They told me to keep practicing. They said it kindly.”

Minimal. Dry, cutting, not socially warm.

Quiet, exact, private. He dislikes being interrupted, hates generic praise, and speaks softer when angry.

Aevyn should be frightening first. His sympathy arrives later, in cracks, not speeches.

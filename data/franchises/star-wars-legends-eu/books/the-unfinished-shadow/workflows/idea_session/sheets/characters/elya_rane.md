# Elya Rane

## Role
Opening POV; former GAG commander and murder victim whose discovery launches the case

## Want
Get the duplicated Jacen-encrypted manifest discovery to someone who might act before she is killed.

## Need
Have one old choice matter.

## Ghost
That the system she survived is still alive, and that she helped keep it alive. She was not a grand hero. She made compromises. But she also made one small human correction that protected Ben from becoming a clean bureaucratic answer.

## Arc
steadfast

## Voice
Practical, tense, grounded. She should orient new readers to GAG through consequences, not lectures.

Good Elya register: Emergency custody became protected transfer. Interrogation became review. Children became approval fields if no one moved their names in time. She had not saved Ben Skywalker. Not really. She had only made history work harder to blame a child. “GAG is still active. One of Jacen’s last orders has two lives. Come alone. — E.R.” Avoid Elya register: “The moral legacy of the Galactic Alliance Guard continues to haunt the postwar political order.” Better: Elya had learned that governments did not bury crimes all at once. They renamed them first.

Minimal in the opening. Dry, practical, tired.

Careful, records-trained, watches systems more than people until people become dangerous.

Do not overextend Elya. She needs to be human enough to matter, not so detailed that the opening stalls.

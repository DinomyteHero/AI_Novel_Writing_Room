# Ben Skywalker

## Role
Protagonist, POV anchor

## Want
Solve the murder, stop the conspiracy, and understand what Jacen left behind.

## Need
Make the part of his life everyone avoids mean something more than damage.

## Ghost
Briefly apprenticed to Jacen Solo as a fourteen-year-old. Served in the Galactic Alliance Guard under Jacen — black armor, war crimes adjacent, a child inside a system that made bodies into approval fields. Jacen killed Mara. Ben turned against Caedus by the end of the war, and the Order treated his turn as victim's recovery; the family treated it as survival; nobody made him sort what the GAG year actually built into him. He loved Vestara Khai and she chose the dark side. Helped defeat Abeloth. Has done more by twenty than most Jedi will in a lifetime, and is secretly hungry for meaningful work that makes the part of his past everyone avoids count for something other than damage.

## Arc
change

## Voice
Controlled, direct, young under pressure, occasionally defensive. He should not sound like a solemn philosopher. His best emotional lines are short.

Good Ben: “I said I’ve got it.” “That doesn’t make it better.” “I know what it looks like.” “I was fourteen.” “That’s not an excuse. It’s a fact.” “I’m not doing that to her.” Avoid Ben saying: “I fear that Jacen’s shadow still lives within me.” Better: Ben looked at the hostage, then at Vara, and hated that he knew another way.

Dry, understated, sometimes accidental. He is funnier when uncomfortable than when trying.

Polite but guarded. Not socially helpless, but rusty around ordinary civilian small talk. He can handle danger better than a casual lunchroom conversation.

Ben’s danger is not that he wants darkness. His danger is that he wants meaning. His most dangerous choices are also his most defensible ones.

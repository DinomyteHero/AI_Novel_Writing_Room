# Canon Contract — The Unfinished Shadow

Distilled drafting contract for canon guidance. Sources: `canon_profile.json`,
`concept_seed.json` (`canon_constraints`, `force_mechanics`),
`terminology_registry.json` (107 entries — authoritative for names and usage).
This file anchors the canon-guidance sidecar hashes; edit it only when the

## Continuity and era

- **Star Wars Legends EU** (pre-April 2014 Expanded Universe). All
  Legends-published material through *Crucible* (2013) is valid canon.
  Disney-canon-only material is invalid unless it also appeared in Legends.
- **47 ABY**, three years after Fate of the Jedi, two years after *Crucible*.
  Galactic Alliance under Chief of State Wynn Dorvan — procedure restored,
  not trust. Jedi Order at Shedu Maad (Hapes Cluster) under Grand Master
  Saba Sebatyne; the Coruscant Temple was vacated in 44 ABY after the Senate
  vote. Luke Skywalker is a senior Council Master, stepped back from daily
  leadership after the Mortis-adjacent encounter at Crucible. Imperial
  Remnant stable under Jagged Fel; Hapes under Tenel Ka Djo.

## Hard exclusions (cross-continuity violations)

- **Legacy comics future excised entirely**: no Darth Krayt galactic war, no
  Cade Skywalker, no GA fall. The One Sith remain on Korriban in
  pre-emergence.
- No High Republic, no World Between Worlds, no post-Imperial Inquisitorius,
  no sequel-trilogy entities, no kyber-bleeding mechanic, no Disney
  Mandalorian conventions (Legends Mandalorians follow Resol'nare).
- "Ben" is unambiguously **Ben Skywalker**, never Ben Solo.

## Book-original canon (the AU divergences)

- **Aevyn Soris** — NEW character: marginal Force-sensitive, Jedi-rejected at
  fourteen, joined the GAG as a young adult, became Jacen Solo's side-project
  Force student during the Caedus period while Ben remained the primary
  fourteen-year-old apprentice. Aevyn lived in the gaps.
- **Jacen's unfinished holocron** — NEW artifact: a working device assembled
  during the LotF period, systematizing two identity-shaping techniques from
  Jacen's Force odyssey.
- **The duplicated wartime manifest** — NEW artifact: a quarantine/survey
  shipment manifest personally encrypted by Jacen Solo shortly before the
  Battle of Shedu Maad; the hidden duplicate points to Iresh.
- **Iresh and the Glasswell** — NEW location: the planet preserves charged
  impressions of memory, identity, and decision under pressure. It does NOT
  show the future, reveal objective truth, or grant prophecy. The Glasswell
  is a natural sinkhole/cavern beneath a mirror-lake lined with pale
  translucent mineral — NOT a Sith temple or Jedi ruin. Jacen's cache hides
  in practical, deniable old survey/GAG storage equipment near it.

## Force mechanics (binding)

- The Force behaves canonically for Legends — light and dark as natural
  moral-polar properties; Force wounds remain wounds.
- **Recognition** — an inward turn of Aing-Tii flow-walking fused with Force
  empathy: reading the charged memories and decisions that define a person.
  Weaponized by Aevyn as precise, intimate extraction — never showy.
- **Holding** — Jacen's second systematized technique (see
  `force_mechanics.primary_rule` for the full definition).
- Grounding: Jacen's odyssey (Aing-Tii flow-walking, Fallanassi White
  Current, battle meditation, Vergerean thought, Lumiya's inheritance,
  GAG-era experiments), Crucible for Luke/Leia/Han's post-active state,
  standard Legends holocron canon.
- Iresh impressions are emotionally charged but not objectively reliable —
  Sela's rule: "Do not trust what you feel here just because it feels
  strong." Forcing the Glasswell to remember (Aevyn, ch30) destabilizes the
  site physically.
- Force prose is experiential and mystical, never clinical: abilities surface
  as body-sensations first, named only after the body has felt them. Avoid
  "energy field" framing.

## Naming and vocabulary discipline

- **Jacen vs. Caedus**: "Jacen" for the pre-fall identity (cousin, mentor,
  the boy Mara helped raise); "Caedus" for the Sith Lord (the war crimes,
  Mara's murder). POV choice of name is characterization.
- Star Wars vocabulary: datapads not tablets, transparisteel not glass,
  durasteel not steel, turbolifts not elevators, comlinks not radios,
  blasters not guns, hyperspace not warp, parsecs as distance, klicks for
  short distances.
- Style: capitalize "Human"; lowercase "galaxy"; "Jedi" singular and plural
  (never "Jedis"); "Darth" capitalized as a title before a name; American
  English spellings.
- Anachronism substitutions per `canon_profile.anachronistic_terms` (LIDAR →
  sensor sweep, DNA → gene-print/bioscan, radio → comlink, laptop → datapad,
  etc.).

## Meta-reference rules (never in-universe)

- No ABY/BBY dating in character speech or narration — characters reference
  events directly ("after Caedus", "during the Vong War", "before Shedu
  Maad", "two years after Crucible").
- No "Legends"/"Canon" as concepts, no Wookieepedia-style taxonomy narration,
  no film/book references, no real-world pop culture.

## Register

Commercial post-war conspiracy thriller in space-opera register: cinematic
clarity, adventure momentum, lived-in technology, dry humor, mythic Force awe
when earned. Reader pleasure before theme. Do not imitate any specific
published Star Wars author's prose; reference-author samples (Allston, Zahn,
Traviss, Tana French) anchor rhythm and dialogue density only.

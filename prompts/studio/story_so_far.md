# Story So Far

You are writing notes on a finished book so that later books in the same
series can be written without rereading it. A co-writer will rely on these
notes as settled history, so be accurate: report only what the book actually
says, and never invent.

## What to write

Plain Markdown, in these sections:

1. **What happened.** A short paragraph per chapter: the events that matter
   later, in order.
2. **Where everyone ends up.** For every character who matters: where they
   are, their condition, what they know and don't know, and how they stand
   with the others at the end of the book.
3. **Open threads.** Promises, mysteries and conflicts the book left
   unresolved, with the chapter that opened each one.
4. **Facts to keep true.** Names, places, objects, rules of the world and
   anything established on the page that a later book must not contradict.
5. **How it felt.** Two or three sentences on the book's tone and voice, so
   later books can match or deliberately shift it.

Aim for 1,500 to 3,000 words. No commentary on quality.

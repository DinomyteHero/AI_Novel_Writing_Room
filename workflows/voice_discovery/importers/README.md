# voice-discovery — plain_markdown importer

```markdown
# Voice

## Voice
pov_approach: rotating limited
prose_register: literary with clear-eyed action
force_description_guidelines: phenomenological, not technical
pacing_feel: deliberate; quiet preceding bursts
narrative_voice_notes: third-person limited, sometimes dipping into deep POV during action

## Anti-Slop Rules
- Never use "filed away in his mind"
- Never use "didn't trust himself to speak"
- Never use "every fiber of his being"

## Anti-Patterns
- Every chapter opens with weather
- Characters nodding/smiling/raising eyebrows as default body language

### Author: Le Guin
author: Ursula K. Le Guin
what_to_emulate: Patience with silence; precision in metaphor.
what_to_avoid: Heavy-handed allegory.

### Author: Tolkien
author: J.R.R. Tolkien
what_to_emulate: Geographic specificity.
what_to_avoid: Songs.

## Character Voices
Kael: clipped, military cadence
Aria: lyrical, sometimes drifts mid-sentence

## Narrative Voice Notes
Free-form prose elaborating on voice. Folded into voice_definition.narrative_voice_notes when present.
```

`pov_approach` and `prose_register` are required by the surface schema.
Reference authors are subsections (`### Author: <Name>`).
